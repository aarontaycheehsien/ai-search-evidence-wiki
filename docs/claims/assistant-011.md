### Case Study of the Implementation of AI Primo Research Assistant (Beta Version) in Academic Libraries in Poland

- **Locator:** Purpose of the Study and Methods, PDF pp. 2-3; non-adopter results and Discussion, pp. 11-13
- **Note:** The authors identified 19 Primo VE libraries among 65 Polish public-university libraries, of which nine had enabled the beta assistant. They report six adopter and eight non-adopter survey responses, with surveys open March 25-31, 2025. Six of eight non-adopters intended to implement it; two had not considered it. Reported reasons for non-adoption included poor functionality, unsatisfactory humanities results, and awaiting a better version. Two respondents mistakenly believed they lacked the necessary discovery infrastructure. These are institutional reports, not verified performance failures or causal estimates of adoption barriers; the small sample limits generalization beyond this setting and period.
