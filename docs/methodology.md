# Methodology

The wiki treats structured source and claim records as its authoritative layer. Every empirical claim should link to one or more identifiable source records, with a relationship label and, where available, a source locator.

Source categories preserve provenance: peer-reviewed studies, preprints, vendor documentation, system documentation, independent experiments, and editorial sources are distinguished. Independent experiments are not silently presented as published research.

Claim statuses are qualitative: supported, mixed, uncertain, contradicted, or provisional. Evidence relationships are supports, contradicts, qualifies, contextual, or unclear. These labels do not imply a numerical confidence score or resolve disagreements automatically.

Generated Markdown is a deterministic view of YAML. Human editorial interpretation is stored separately and requires explicit human direction to change.
