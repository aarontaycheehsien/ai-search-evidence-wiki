## Bibliographic information

- **Record type:** journal-article
- **Notes:** Evaluated ChatGPT-4o, Claude 3 Sonnet, and Gemini 1.5 Pro on 36 trials from five sepsis guideline questions. Background-data no-error rates ranged from 81.6% to 92.4%; outcome-data no-error rates ranged from 27.8% to 80.7%. Missing or incorrect values accounted for most errors. Chain-of-thought and self-reflection prompts changed accuracy only modestly; self-reflection increased processing time. Reproducibility was lower for outcome extraction than for background fields.
