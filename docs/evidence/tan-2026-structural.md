- **Notes:** Benchmarked GPT-5.2 and Qwen3-VL on 52 manually annotated empirical papers across five domains. Macro F1 was 0.18-0.35 overall; performance declined from single-attribute extraction to relational tuples, and both models had F1=0 for the full meta-analytic association tuple. Long-context inputs often worsened performance. The benchmark includes broad scholarly evidence extraction rather than only clinical review studies; predictions were scored against manually curated annotations with a semantic matching step.
