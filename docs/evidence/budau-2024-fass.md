- **Notes:** Benchmarked ChatGPT-generated PubMed Boolean queries against automated and expert queries across 111 COVID-19 reviews, including 62 with manual queries. Zero-seed ChatGPT recall@1000 was 0.2594 versus 0.3581 for manual queries; three seed studies raised ChatGPT recall@1000 to 0.3300. The corpus was restricted to COVID-19 and PubMed-indexed studies.
