- **Notes:** In a data-extraction arm nested within a registered review, a customized GPT-4o extracted fields from 15 SLE metabolomics studies. Overall template concordance with human consensus was 25%; country extraction matched for 15/15 studies, while age and sex-distribution fields matched for 0/15. Mean model extraction time was 5.7 minutes per study versus 30.4 minutes for human reviewers. The sample was small and disease-specific; table and supplementary information were frequent failure points. This paper also contains a separate risk-of-bias arm that is outside the extraction results recorded here.
