- **Source category:** peer-reviewed-study
- **Record type:** journal-article
- **Notes:** Preregistered validation of four LLMs against published human judgments for 100 RoB 1 and 100 RoB 2 trial assessments. Agreement was fair for RoB 1 and poor for RoB 2, with low positive and negative predictive values and systematic over-flagging of concerns. The human reference judgments may themselves be imperfect, sampling was not random, and training-data contamination could not be excluded.
