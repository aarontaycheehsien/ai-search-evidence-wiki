# Human vs. machine in medical search strategy development: a comparative evaluation of ChatGPT-4.1

## Bibliographic information

- **Authors:** J. Walz
- **Year:** 2025
- **DOI:** 10.32384/jeahil21697
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.32384/jeahil21697
- **Publication status:** Published in Journal of EAHIL 21(4), 18-21
- **Source category:** peer-reviewed-study
- **Record type:** journal-article
- **Notes:** Compared six ChatGPT-4.1 MEDLINE strategies across two Cochrane topics with published expert strategies using PRESS. Five of six retrieved all benchmark included articles, but hallucinated MeSH terms appeared in five of six and syntax/display errors required repair. Only two topics and one reviewer were used; precision and time savings were not quantified.
