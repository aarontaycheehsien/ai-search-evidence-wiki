- **Notes:** Compared Elicit with a completed dermatology systematic review containing 24 randomized trials. Elicit's 59-result concept search recovered one included trial, while its 27-result keyword search recovered none. In extraction, disease was correct for all 24 trials, but study country and derived Jadad scores were wrong in 5 of 24 cases each. The authors found raw extraction faster but reported that cross-checking and custom-column setup could erase the time gain. This was one narrow review using one evolving product.
