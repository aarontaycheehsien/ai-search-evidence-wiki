# A critical evaluation of generative query expansion on biomedical literature retrieval

## Bibliographic information

- **Authors:** Yilu Fang, Gongbo Zhang, Fangyi Chen, Yifan Peng, Chunhua Weng
- **Year:** 2026
- **DOI:** 10.1093/jamia/ocag037
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jamia/ocag037
- **Publication status:** Published in Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association 33(6), 1121-1133
- **Source category:** peer-reviewed-study
- **Record type:** journal-article
- **Notes:** Benchmarked eight generative query-expansion methods with three LLMs across five biomedical retrieval datasets and a roughly 36-million-record PubMed corpus. Expansion sometimes improved TREC Precision Medicine retrieval but often reduced BioASQ and PubMedQA performance. Effects depended more on the expansion method and evidence type than on the backbone model, and largely changed ranking rather than screening coverage.
