- **Notes:** Applied five gold-validated verifiers to identical claim-citation pairs from agentic scientific-synthesis outputs. The measured unsupported-citation rate ranged from about 3% to about 18% as verifier strictness changed. On 300 cited sentences, verifiers agreed strongly on supported citations but only weakly on which citations to flag (negative-specific agreement 0.27-0.30). The study concerns multi-paper cited answers and is a preprint; it also shows that verifier-dependent rates should not be compared without a shared protocol.
