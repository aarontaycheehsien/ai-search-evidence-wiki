- **Notes:** Fine-tuned Mistral-Instruct-7B on PROSPERO search queries and evaluated it on 57 reviews. Median sensitivity was 85% with title-only input and 86% with title plus key questions; respective median numbers needed to read were 1,206 and 908. Eight librarian interviews concluded that generated queries needed scrutiny and were better suited to scoping or initial drafts. Training queries were protocol searches and did not necessarily reflect final expert strategies.
