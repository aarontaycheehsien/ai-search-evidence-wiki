# Benchmarking Human-AI Collaboration for Common Evidence Appraisal Tools

## Bibliographic information

- **Authors:** Tim Woelfle, Julian Hirt, Perrine Janiaud, Ludwig Kappos, John P. A. Ioannidis, Lars G. Hemkens
- **Year:** 2024
- **DOI:** 10.1101/2024.04.21.24306137
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.04.21.24306137
- **Publication status:** medRxiv preprint posted 22 April 2024; not peer reviewed
- **Source category:** preprint
- **Record type:** preprint
- **Notes:** Compared five LLMs, combinations of LLMs, and human-LLM pairs with human consensus for PRISMA, AMSTAR, and PRECIS-2 ratings. Standalone LLMs performed below individual humans. Human-LLM agreement workflows improved accuracy for PRISMA and AMSTAR while deferring a minority of items, but PRECIS-2 required deferral for roughly three quarters of ratings. This is a preprint and the workload savings were modeled rather than prospectively measured.
