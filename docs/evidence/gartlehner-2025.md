- **Notes:** Prospective study within six ongoing reviews comparing Claude-first extraction checked by a human with conventional human-first extraction checked by a second human. Across 9,341 data items from 63 studies, the AI-assisted workflow had 91.0% accuracy versus 89.0% for human-only extraction and saved a median 41 minutes per study. Both arms retained errors, and only discordant items were adjudicated, so shared errors could have been missed.
