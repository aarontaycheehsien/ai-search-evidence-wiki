- **Notes:** Retrospectively compared Lens.org, SpiderCite, and Microsoft Copilot with customary information-specialist searches across seven health-technology assessment projects, followed by a focus group with seven information specialists. Average sensitivity across six review projects was 0.676 for Lens.org, 0.230-0.257 for the two SpiderCite directions, and 0.244 for Copilot, versus 0.986 for the reference searches. Test searches took less execution and modeled screening time, but their suitability varied by task. Searchers knew the target studies, the project sample was small, and tools were not evaluated in combination.
