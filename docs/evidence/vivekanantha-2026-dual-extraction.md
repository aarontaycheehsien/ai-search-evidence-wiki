# Automated data extraction for systematic reviews using GPT-5.2 and Google Gemini Pro 3: A dual-large language model approach in orthopaedic research

## Bibliographic information

- **Authors:** Prushoth Vivekanantha, Harjind Kahlon, Oluwatoba T. Balogun, Marc Daniel Bouchard, Darren de Sa, Olufemi R. Ayeni, Jeffrey Kay
- **Year:** 2026

- **Publication status:** Published in Knee Surgery, Sports Traumatology, Arthroscopy 34, 3011-3029
- **Source category:** peer-reviewed-study
- **Record type:** journal-article
- **Notes:** Tested GPT-5.2 and Gemini 3 Pro on 384 fields from eight studies in one paediatric revision ACLR review. Both models were fully correct on 82.0% of fields; at least one model was fully correct on 95.1%. Extraction took 27.0 and 35.8 minutes respectively, at a combined reported API cost of $3.22. The sample was narrow, the human adjudicator was not blinded to model output, and the authors describe the approach as a first pass requiring human review.
