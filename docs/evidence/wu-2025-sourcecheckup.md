# An automated framework for assessing how well LLMs cite relevant medical references

## Bibliographic information

- **Authors:** Kevin Wu, Eric Wu, Kevin Wei, Angela Zhang, Allison Casasola, Teresa Nguyen, Sith Riantawan, Patricia Shi, Daniel Ho, James Zou
- **Year:** 2025
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-025-58551-6

- **Publication status:** Published in Nature Communications 16:3615
- **Source category:** peer-reviewed-study
- **Record type:** journal-article
- **Notes:** Evaluated seven LLMs on 800 medical questions and nearly 58,000 statement-source pairs. Between 50% and 90% of responses were not fully supported by their cited sources; GPT-4o with web search had 55% fully supported responses. The automated verifier agreed with physician consensus on 88.7% of 400 annotated pairs, and physicians confirmed 105 of 110 pairs that the verifier marked unsupported. This is adjacent evidence from medical question answering rather than literature-review generation.
