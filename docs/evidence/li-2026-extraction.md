# What level of automation is 'good enough'? A benchmark of large language models for meta-analysis data extraction

- **Notes:** Benchmarked GPT-4o-mini, Gemini 2.0 Flash, and Grok 3 on 58 RCTs from six meta-analyses. Precision was generally high, but omissions dominated the 22,196 catalogued error instances. Customized prompts increased recall by an average 14.8 percentage points with a small average precision decrease. The authors recommend task-specific automation with human verification rather than autonomous extraction, especially for statistical results.
