- **Notes:** Reproducibility study of LLM-generated PubMed Boolean queries using CLEF TAR and seed-study benchmarks. Results varied materially by model, prompt, validation, and seed choice. Generated queries generally had higher precision but lower recall than expert manual queries; combining guided queries could approach manual recall while sharply reducing precision. The authors flag possible benchmark contamination and the post-hoc nature of best-seed choice.
