# Evaluating the accuracy and speed of eight deduplication tools: A comparative study

## Bibliographic information

- **Authors:** Sarah Bateup, Helen Fulbright, Klas Moberg, Kaitlyn Hair, Emmy Peterson, Claire M. Stansfield, Riaz Qureshi, Justin Clark
- **Year:** 2026

- **Publication status:** Published in Research Synthesis Methods
- **Source category:** peer-reviewed-study
- **Record type:** journal-article
- **Notes:** Compared eight deduplication tools against dual-reviewer gold standards from five rerun Cochrane searches. Across 22,778 records, false removal of unique records ranged from 2 to 22, retained duplicates from 34 to 280, and total processing time from 42 seconds to 20 hours 34 minutes. No tool was best on every outcome; the sample was limited to five biomedical review datasets.
