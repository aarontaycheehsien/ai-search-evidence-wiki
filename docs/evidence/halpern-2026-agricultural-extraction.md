- **Notes:** A single Claude Opus 4.6 agent extracted numerical effects across five agricultural meta-analysis datasets: 1,149 observations matched from 136 papers. The paper reports proportional TOST equivalence in all five datasets and correlations of 0.984-0.999. Important qualification: LLM-based alignment was used to match extracted observations to reference rows, and materially changed agreement metrics without changing extracted values. Some validation data informed pipeline refinement; variance extraction was not formally validated, and individual-level agreement was wider than aggregate agreement.
