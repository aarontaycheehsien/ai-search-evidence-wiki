Retrospective evaluation of GPT-4o mini, Llama 3.1 8B, and Gemma 2 9B across three reviews (1,757, 21,116, and 7,693 records), using prespecified rating thresholds and a majority-vote ensemble. Against full-text inclusions, majority voting achieved 100% sensitivity in all three reviews; workload saving at 100% sensitivity ranged from 12.5% to 88.5%. Positive predictive values remained low. Results are retrospective, and the small number of review datasets limits generalizability.
