- **Notes:** Compared 2,169 AI-generated clinical reviews with 62 reviews from four leading medical journals. Reference accuracy was the percentage of cited references whose explanation in a review was manually judged consistent with the original article. The pooled AI median was 100% (IQR 73.55-100%) versus 100% (IQR 100-100%) for human reviews; standalone LLMs had a median of 95% (IQR 51.22-100%). Results varied substantially across systems and generation workflows, and the study did not cover all clinical fields.
