- **Publication status:** Published in BMC Medical Research Methodology 26:33
- **Source category:** peer-reviewed-study
- **Record type:** journal-article
- **Notes:** Compared ChatGPT-4 and Claude 3 Opus with verified gold-standard datasets for 105 RCTs from two reviews. Binary-outcome group-size accuracy was 91%-94%, event-count accuracy 57%-71%; continuous-outcome group-size accuracy was 59%-69%, while mean and standard-deviation accuracy ranged from 24%-56%. Supplementary files were excluded, prompting variants did not significantly change accuracy, and the sample was limited to two reviews.
