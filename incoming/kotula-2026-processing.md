# Kotuła and Gmiterek (2026): processing audit

Processed 2026-09-27 from the complete local PDF:
`incoming/papers/Kotula and Gmiterek - 2026 - Primo Research Assistant implementation study.pdf`.
Read all 22 pages; visually checked Figures 3-5 on PDF pages 9-11.

## Classifications

- **NEW — assistant-011:** March 2025 implementation snapshot and institutional
  reports of non-adoption barriers. Nine of 19 technically eligible libraries
  had enabled the assistant; surveys received six adopter and eight non-adopter
  responses. Scope is Polish public-university libraries, not all libraries.
- **NEW — assistant-012:** Perceived search/service benefits, trust and metadata
  concerns, missing user/staff evaluation information, and doubts about meeting
  users' needs. These are director reports, not direct user testing.
- **QUALIFIES — workload interpretation:** The abstract and discussion emphasize
  reduced workload, but Figure 4 (p. 9) shows zero workload-reduction selections
  and one work-acceleration selection. The source and claim notes retain both;
  no measured efficiency claim is inferred.
- **NO CHANGE — assistant-002:** The study does not benchmark relevance, recall,
  or coverage against conventional search. Its perceptions do not confirm or
  contradict Galbreath et al.'s measured relevance comparison.
- **NO CHANGE — assistant-007:** There is no timed comparison or sensitivity
  estimate. The reported benefits do not establish measured efficiency gains.
- **NO CHANGE — assistant-006 and assistant-008:** These concern Elicit-specific
  user-facing evaluations and researcher think-aloud behavior respectively;
  this library-director survey is represented separately.

## Limits and unresolved reporting issues

Several adopter results and charts reflect five answers although the authors
report six adopter survey responses overall. Preserve item-level denominators.
The methods also say the preliminary-interview library was excluded from the
adopter survey invitations while using nine adopters as the response-rate
denominator. No denominator correction is inferred.

The discussion's statement that only one library had implemented the tool as
of October 28, 2025 (p. 12) is ambiguous alongside the March snapshot of nine
implementations. It is not treated as a later total or as one additional adoption.

Product descriptions and roadmap statements are background documentation,
not independently measured capabilities. Literature cited in this paper has
not been added as evidence without reading the original sources.
